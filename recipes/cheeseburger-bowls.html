Cheeseburger Bowls

Description

These cheeseburger bowls capture everything you love about a classic cheeseburger in a fork-friendly form. Crispy fries act as the base, soaking up juicy, seasoned beef with hints of mustard and ketchup. The creamy, tangy sauce ties it all together, while fresh lettuce, tomatoes, and pickles add crunch and brightness. It is savory, slightly sweet, and satisfying with every bite.

Ingredients

  • 1 (28 ounce) package frozen French fries
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 teaspoons sweet pickle relish
  • 1 teaspoon apple cider vinegar
  • 1/4 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 3 tablespoons yellow mustard, divided
  • 3 tablespoons ketchup, divided
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt, divided
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder, divided
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder, divided
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 pound 80/20 ground beef
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 4 cups chopped iceberg lettuce
  • 1 1/2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 cup diced dill pickles
  • 1 cup shredded Cheddar cheese

Steps

  1. Prepare fries according to package directions until golden brown and crisp, about 20 minutes.
  2. While fries cook, whisk together mayonnaise, relish, vinegar, paprika, 2 tablespoons mustard, 2 tablespoons ketchup, 1/2 teaspoon salt, 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder, and 1/2 teaspoon onion powder in a bowl; set aside.
  3. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add ground beef in large pieces; sprinkle with black pepper and remaining 1/2 teaspoon each salt, garlic powder, and onion powder. Cook, undisturbed, until deeply browned on the bottom, about 3 minutes. Turn and cook, breaking into large crumbles and stirring occasionally, until browned and cooked through, 2 to 3 more minutes. Stir in remaining 1 tablespoon each mustard and ketchup; cook, stirring gently, until coated and slightly caramelized, about 1 minute. Remove from heat.
  4. Divide hot fries evenly among 4 shallow bowls, arranging as a base. Spoon hot beef mixture into one section of each bowl. Arrange lettuce, tomatoes, and pickles in separate sections around beef. Sprinkle evenly with Cheddar cheese and drizzle evenly with reserved sauce, to taste. Serve immediately with more sauce on the side.
