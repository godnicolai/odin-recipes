Crispy Pork and Peanut Noodles

Description

These crispy pork and peanut noodles come together in about 10 minutes for a quick and easy weeknight meal. Ground pork, ramen noodles, and a spicy peanut sauce create the perfect balance of sweet, salty, and spicy flavors in every bite.

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 2 (3-ounce) packages ramen noodles, seasoning packet discarded
  • 1 pound ground pork
  • 3 medium green onions, sliced, light and dark parts separated
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 2 tablespoons peanut butter
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons chili garlic sauce
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup water, or as needed

Steps

  1. Bring a pot of water to a boil and add ramen noodles. Cook until just underdone, about 2 ½ minutes; drain.
  2. Meanwhile, heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Heat oil then add pork and cook crumbling until browned and crispy, 3 to 4 minutes. Add in lighter parts of the green onion and garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
  3. Stir together peanut butter, ground ginger, soy sauce, chili garlic sauce, and brown sugar in a small bowl until well combined. Pour peanut sauce over pork mixture. Stir in noodles until coated. Add water to loosen the sauce as desired and cook for 1 minute. Remove from heat and top with remaining green onions.
